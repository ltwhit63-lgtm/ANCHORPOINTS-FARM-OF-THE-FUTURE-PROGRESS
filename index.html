Growing Together: Securing the Ground for a Community Farm • Oklahoma

Why this land matters to all of us

This is a community project, and it starts with the ground beneath it. AnchorPoints Aquaponics is planned as Oklahoma’s first-of-its-kind aquaponics farm and training center: a place for fresh, locally grown food, hands-on education, workforce training, and opportunities for young people, families, and neighbors.

Your gift to this campaign goes toward securing the land so this work can begin. Once the land is fully secured, we can move on to development and the financing to build an ADA-accessible, solar-powered, energy-efficient facility that serves the whole community. (Donations here are for the land only, not for construction.)

The pictures below show the land today and the vision it is growing toward. Each gift brings that vision a little closer for everyone it will serve.

Learning, Opportunity & Growth for Everyone

Once the land is secured, AnchorPoints is planned as an education and community center, not just a farm. We are also eager to partner with colleges and offer internships to their students. Here is what we hope to offer.

Youth

Hands-on, real-world learning that makes science and agriculture exciting.

  • Aquaponics and STEM education
  • Youth agricultural leadership programs
  • Mentoring and confidence-building
  • Outdoor classrooms and community gardens

Young Adults

Practical skills and a path to good work in a growing field.

  • Workforce training and job readiness
  • AI and technology in agriculture
  • Entrepreneur development
  • Climate-controlled, year-round growing skills

College Students

Real-world experience that builds résumés and careers.

  • Internships and hands-on training in aquaponics, AI, and sustainable agriculture
  • Partnerships with colleges and universities
  • Our hope: partner colleges will offer credit hours for students who take part
  • Research and innovation projects

The Whole Community

A welcoming place where neighbors learn, gather, and share.

  • Fresh, locally grown food and family food production
  • Classes, events, and a community meeting hall
  • An ADA-accessible campus open to everyone
  • A model other communities can learn from

Programs shown are part of our planned vision and may grow and change as the project develops. College credit and partnerships depend on agreements with each school.
